How picks work

Spreads and over/unders where our number differs from DraftKings. Rules come from two seasons of backtests plus this season's live results. 🔥 STRONG = underdog with an edge of 5–12 (backtest ~56%). 📉 Under (1u) = an under where our total is 5+ points below DraftKings', both teams 4+ D-I games; our totals run a little high, so unders have won (41-25 live) and overs haven't. 📈 Over (0.5u) = an over where our total is 8+ points above DraftKings' (36-21 live); smaller over edges are mostly the model running high and stay Watch. Favorites are Watch only (the old Lean tier was 19-18). ⭐ Best Bet = STRONG + High: underdog, edge 8–12, both teams 4+ D-I games (~58–62%). Everything else is 👀 Watch: edges of 2.5–5, spreads of 20+, young or big-edge favorites (they lost), and edges of 12+, which usually mean the model is missing something (like blowouts of non-D-I teams). Confidence = expected win rate of the pick's group: High 57%+, Medium 55–57%, Low under 55%. Green rows are Best Bets. Bet size: ⭐ Best Bets 2u, other 🔥 STRONG 1u, 📉 Unders 1u, 📈 Overs 0.5u, 🏀 Big Games and Watch not bet. 🏀 Big Game (tracked only since 11/27, when they were 14-15) = both teams from the ACC, Big Ten, Big 12, SEC or Big East with an edge of 3–12 on a spread under 20. These are for games worth watching: the backtest was only about break-even plus 4% (54%), so they're kept out of the model's record and tracked on their own line. Projections account for injuries: players ESPN lists as out (and regulars who sat out a team's last game) are taken off that team's number before the edge is figured. When to bet: 7:30–9 AM, right after the morning run, to get the early number. Line now is DraftKings' current line, checked at 11 AM, 1, 4, 6 and 8 PM: ⚠ = moved 1+ point against us since posting (skip it if you haven't bet yet), ✅ = moved our way. Totals stay Watch-only. First-half spreads ("1H") use DraftKings' real first-half line when available (marked "DK"), otherwise an estimate ("est."); our fair number is in brackets; tracked, not counted. 52.4% breaks even at –110.

🎰 Parlay of the day

ℹ️ How it works

Picked in the morning and kept all day from the big games (at least one ACC, Big Ten, Big 12, SEC or Big East team, spread of 15 or less): 3 legs when 3 games have a 4+ point edge on DraftKings, otherwise the 2 best edges. Each leg is an alt spread 6 points better than DK's line, or a favorite's moneyline when DK's price is good. Over two past seasons the 3-leg version hit about half the time (paying around +150) and the 2-leg version about 60% (around –120). Fair = the price where a leg breaks even; a leg is worth it at that price or better. ✅ / ⚠ = the line moved our way / against us (⚠ legs offer swaps). Stake: 0.25–0.5u.

🚀 Longshot parlay

ℹ️ How it works

A second, riskier parlay for bigger odds; the Parlay of the day above doesn't change. It takes the 4 best edges among today's big games (same games as above) as alt spreads 3 points better than DraftKings' line, usually around +450 to +600. Over two past seasons it hit about 1 day in 5 (15% and 24%). Only on days with 4 such games. Stake small: 0.1–0.25u.
